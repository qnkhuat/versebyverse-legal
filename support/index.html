Verse by Verse — Support

Questions, feedback, or a problem with the app? Email Hohama Studio at admin@hohama.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

The candle keeps going out. The candle burns while you are not reading. Each new verse you reach adds fuel. To turn Candle mode off, tap the flame button at the top of the reader, or use Settings.

A question looks wrong. Questions are written by AI from the King James text and can contain mistakes. Email us the chapter and the question, and we will fix it.

I did not get my daily reminder. Check that notifications are allowed for Verse by Verse in the iOS Settings app, and that the reminder is on in the app's Settings.
