Verse by Verse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Verse by Verse ("the app") is provided by HAPIGA VIET NAM COMPANY LIMITED (Hohama Studio, "we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, or location. We do not receive your reading history. The app has no account for you to create, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Reading works fully offline. Candle questions use the internet and a random, anonymous app identifier (see below).

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of data on your device so it can work as you expect:

  • Your reading position (book, chapter, and verse).
  • Your reading preferences (text size and page theme).
  • Your daily reminder setting and reminder time.
  • Whether you have completed the welcome screens.

This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. Your device's own backup service (for example, iCloud Backup) can include it, under that service's terms.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks iOS for permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your device. No push token or device identifier is sent to us or to any server. You can turn the reminder off in the app's Settings, or turn off notifications for the app in iOS Settings.

Candle questions

When Candle mode loads questions about a chapter, the app sends the book and chapter number (for example, "Genesis 1") to our server. The server, hosted on Google Cloud (Firebase), asks an AI service (Replicate) to write the questions, then stores them so every reader of that chapter gets the same questions.

To keep this service for the app only, the app signs in to Firebase Authentication anonymously the first time it loads questions. Firebase creates a random identifier for your installation of the app. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple account, or device, and we use it only to confirm that a request comes from the app. It is not used for advertising or tracking. Your reading history, answers, and settings are not sent with it.

Like any internet request, the request reaches our server from your IP address, and our hosting provider may keep it in standard server logs for a short time. We do not use it to identify you.

Questions are written by AI from the King James text and can contain mistakes.

Sharing verses

When you share a verse, the app opens the standard iOS share sheet. The app you choose to share with (for example, Messages or Mail) receives the verse text, under that app's own privacy terms. We do not receive a copy.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. Apart from the requests described above, it does not send data over the internet.

Children

The app does not ask children for personal details. Candle questions use the same anonymous identifier process for all readers, as described above.

Scripture text

The Bible text is the King James Version, which is in the public domain.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will also describe it in the app's release notes.
